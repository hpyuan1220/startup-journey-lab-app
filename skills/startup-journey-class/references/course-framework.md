# Startup Journey Lab course framework

## Ten-week evidence path

| Week | Topic | Core submission |
|---|---|---|
| 1 | Course orientation and startup starting point | Individual starting card |
| 2 | Problem exploration and selection | Individual problem list and selection reason |
| 3 | User interviews | Interview plan, verbatim quotes, and insights |
| 4 | AI native company design | AI workflow, data, human checks, and risks |
| 5 | Product and MVP | Team selection record and testable MVP |
| 6 | First users | Recruitment and user-test records |
| 7 | Product data and retention | Behavior plot and iteration decision |
| 8 | Business model and pricing | Payer, value, cost, price, and validation plan |
| 9 | Team collaboration | Team charter and contribution record |
| 10 | Launch and demo | Five-minute demo, portfolio, and reflection |

## Standard 120-minute rhythm

- 0–10: connect prior evidence to the weekly completion condition.
- 10–25: selected video excerpts and three to five usable judgments.
- 25–45: concrete case with correct and incorrect examples.
- 45–55: demonstrate the task, worksheet, and web fields.
- 55–65: break.
- 65–95: individual or team production.
- 95–110: peer check and revision.
- 110–120: completeness check, submission, and next action.

## Required deliverable alignment

The PPTX and PDF reading version must contain identical slide content. The lesson plan, slides, worksheet, and web app must use the same labels and submission rule. Each weekly page should expose the weekly blueprint, video highlights, separate material links, interactive work, completion state, save or restore behavior, preview, and submission.

## Acceptance checks

- A student can understand what to do without an oral correction.
- Every conclusion can point to observation, quote, event, test, or sourced data.
- Examples illustrate a method without becoming the only acceptable answer.
- Student data is accessible only under the intended student and teacher permissions.
- Fifty-student operation, reload recovery, mobile layout, exports, and error feedback are checked.
- The weekly material states what remains uncertain and how the next week will test it.
